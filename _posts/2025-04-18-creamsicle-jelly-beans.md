I'm back with another capsule review before
the more substantial final reviews of the season drop this weekend.
Today we're taking a look at Creamsicle Jelly Beans,
inspired by the classic Good Humor ice cream treat
that marries an orange sherbet popsicle with vanilla ice cream.

This one will be short and sweet for a few reasons:

1. Like the [Fruity Pebbles Jelly Beans](/fruity-pebbles-jelly-beans),
   these are also manufactured by Flix Candy
   and their physical characteristics are identical.
2. While they have two colors, orange and white, in the bag,
   I cannot be 100% sure they are actually two different flavors.
3. I've never been a big fan of Creamsicles, anyway.

As far as how they actually taste, they're not bad,
but I think they lean too heavily on the vanilla
and the orange is too understated.
If I ate some without knowing what they were or seeing the color of them,
I'd be hard pressed to identify the orange flavor.

If you're really into Creamsicles, I suppose they're worth checking out,
but this is not a bag I'll be returning to in the future.
